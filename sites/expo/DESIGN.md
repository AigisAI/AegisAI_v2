## Version C — security sense quiz

`/expo3` is independently designed from a new brief. A saturated orange canvas, oversized black-and-white typography, a numbered circular counter and a paper quiz sheet replace the previous compositions. Visitors make choices in three fictional situations involving secret handling, authorization and query parameters. Immediate explanations lead to a three-point learning recap and the existing anonymous interest and sharing actions. The score is educational, not a professional assessment or security diagnosis. No image generation or Stitch was used for this version.
